spotlight · brain teasers

market-sizing drills that grade the reasoning.

fermi estimation, with walkthroughs

the number's fine — the process is the answer.

Fifteen market-sizing and Fermi-style drills. You submit a number, the app checks it against a tolerance window, and then reveals the line-by-line walkthrough so you can compare your assumptions to a strong one. Anchor to the real-world figure is shown at the end.

  • Numeric answer + generous tolerance window (estimation is the skill)
  • Per-step reasoning walkthrough — the part interviewers actually grade
  • Real-world anchor at the end so you know how close you were
  • Two categories: market-sizing ($) and Fermi (count)
brain teaser · bt-m1
Prompt: Estimate the annual US retail coffee-shop market ($B).
1
US population ≈ 330M; adults ≈ 260M.
2
~65% of adults drink coffee → ~170M coffee drinkers.
3
Of those, ~45% regularly buy coffee out → ~75M shop buyers.
4
Frequency: 4 cups/week × 50 weeks × $3.50 ≈ $700/yr.
5
your guess: $52B· anchor: $47B · within tolerance ✓
