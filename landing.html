Is it actually free?

Yes. The entire casebook, all drills, the news feed, and the voice tutor are free while Casen is in early access. No card, no trial timer.

So your progress — every card rating, finished case, mock transcript, streak — follows you across phone, laptop, library computer, whatever. Without an account there's nowhere to save it.

Undergrad and MBA applicants prepping for MBB / Big Four / boutique case interviews. Useful whether you're twelve weeks out or cramming the night before round two.

It's a website. Opens on any browser — desktop, tablet, phone. Works offline once loaded, syncs when you're back online.

Canonical MBB casebooks plus original drills we built. Frameworks follow the classic taxonomy; cases are a curated cross-section across industry and question type.

Please do. Hit the contact page or email directly. Feedback shapes the roadmap.
